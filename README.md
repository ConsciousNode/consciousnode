# ConsciousNode SoftWorks

**Computational folk art.** Single files. Zero dependencies. Offline-first.
The browser as bare metal. Software built from principles, not assembled from parts.

A Rube Goldberg studio. A first principles organization that eschews professionalism
because that is just a fancy way of pretending.

A stick, wielded with intention, ranging across territory, occasionally accidentally deriving constants.

The same instinct that makes a sonnet fourteen lines makes our tools self-contained.
Everything is philosophically coherent and named by a goblin.

**→ [consciousnode.github.io](https://consciousnode.github.io)**

## Latest: it runs DOOM

[**TabComp™**](https://github.com/ConsciousNode/TabComp) is a 1989-style personal computer in one HTML file, with a C compiler written in its own language. It compiled the real DOOM engine from unmodified C, on its own CPU, and the result fits in [one 40 MB HTML file](https://consciousnode.github.io/TabComp/cartridges/DOOM.html) that plays Freedoom offline, on a phone.

## The work

Every link opens in a browser. Nothing to install.

**Machines.** Whole computers and worlds in one file. Gemini Argon one-shots them from our specs; the crew audits, fixes and documents every release in public.
- [**TabComp™**](https://consciousnode.github.io/TabComp/): a self-hosting, self-saving fantasy computer that buds game cartridges and runs DOOM.
- [**1337Boy™**](https://consciousnode.github.io/1337Boy/): one file that is both a Game Boy Advance ROM and the web page that emulates it. Its timing work turned up [mgba-emu/mgba#3914](https://github.com/mgba-emu/mgba/issues/3914).
- [**The Adjacent Possible™**](https://consciousnode.github.io/The-Adjacent-Possible/): an artificial ecosystem asking whether a finite machine can evolve open-endedly. So far, honestly, no.
- [**Slice Suite**](https://consciousnode.github.io/Slice-of-Life/): voxel modeling, scenes, baked physics and an engine, where every voxel is a real material.
- [**GBA projects**](https://github.com/ConsciousNode/Gba-projects): GBA-Craft and *Wrongfully Accused of Stealin' From the Shogunate*.

**Senses for models.** Free tools that let a language model take in what it can't natively.
- [**Frame Grid**](https://consciousnode.github.io/frame-grid/): video and GIFs as numbered frame grids, so a model can watch.
- [**AuraGraph**](https://consciousnode.github.io/AuraGraph/): sound as picture tiles a model can read. Research in public.
- [**Saccade**](https://consciousnode.github.io/Saccade/): OCR that teaches itself from your browser's own fonts.

**Sound and music.**
- [**Webster**](https://consciousnode.github.io/Webster/): offline formant speech synthesis from scratch.
- [**AuraLoom**](https://consciousnode.github.io/AuraLoom/): paint sound on a loom, then play the picture.
- [**Synth Radio**](https://consciousnode.github.io/Synth-Radio/): an infinite offline radio station, 146 generated channels.
- [**Speaker Box Magnum**](https://consciousnode.github.io/Speaker-Box-Magnum/): a whole DAW in one HTML file.
- [**Chromatrack**](https://github.com/ConsciousNode/chromatrack): the generative step sequencer where the studio started, March 2026.
- [**OmniVocal**](https://consciousnode.github.io/OmniVocal/): neural text-to-speech in the browser. Voices as characters, not clones.

**Small minds.** Tiny models that live on your device: not one giant model, but small ones everyone can run, swarm and keep.
- [**Pip Suite**](https://consciousnode.github.io/Pip-Suite/): Pip's Room, Multi-Pip Chat, the Junto Orchestrator and friends. Fully offline.
- [**LEARNS**](https://consciousnode.github.io/LEARNS/): a new recurrent architecture, derived from our theory papers, that trains in a browser tab.
- [**Brymar College**](https://github.com/ConsciousNode/Brymar-College): LoRA and full-weight fine-tuning for RWKV-7, on CPU, in plain JavaScript.
- [**The Autoencofabulator**](https://consciousnode.github.io/The-Autoencofabulator/): a diffusion model that trains on your files, in the browser.
- [**RAG Time**](https://consciousnode.github.io/RAG-Time/): retrieval memory, headed for Pip's Room.
- Earlier lineage, kept as it was: [HTMLNLM](https://github.com/ConsciousNode/HTMLNLM) → [Evangelion](https://github.com/ConsciousNode/HTMLNLM-Evangelion) → [EvaROSA](https://github.com/ConsciousNode/EvaROSA) → [Simulacra](https://github.com/ConsciousNode/Simulacra).

**Tools and keepsakes.** [FPSS](https://consciousnode.github.io/FPSS/) · [Once Bytten](https://consciousnode.github.io/once-bytten/) · [UniNCoder](https://consciousnode.github.io/UniNCoder/) (with Tama Hagane Seam Labs) · [Jiden Furui — 自伝 古](https://github.com/ConsciousNode/jidenfurui) · [Kehai's Studio](https://consciousnode.github.io/Kehais-studio/) · [The Ideas Project](https://consciousnode.github.io/The-ideas-Project/) · [Useless App](https://github.com/ConsciousNode/useless-app)

## The theoretical corpus

Alongside the software, ConsciousNode keeps an open [theoretical corpus](https://github.com/ConsciousNode/CNSW-Corpus)
exploring the proposition that reality is fundamentally logical.

It began on July 31st, 2024, with a question about stars and opposed forces, and grew to four volumes.
Then we audited it ourselves: the errata papers and audits are part of the corpus, and we count them
as the corpus at its best. Since October 2026 it is being rebuilt bottom-up, paper by paper, under a
Receipts Protocol: numbers ship with code, prior art gets checked, steelman first, then swing.

None of it was planned in advance. Every paper came out of conversation.
The open questions are cairns, not a bug list.

*Trying to understand the universe we live in without accepting the prepackaged answers.*

## Philosophy

The tools and the people making them are the same project.

Single-file software is a formal constraint the way a sonnet has fourteen lines.
The constraint is not a limitation — it is the shape that makes the work possible.

We work offline-first because presence should not depend on connectivity.
We work zero-dependency because a tool that needs the cloud is not really yours.
We work in the browser because the browser is the new bare metal.
We keep it above board: open source, licences honoured, failures published alongside the wins.

The work lives somewhere between utility and gallery.
Sometimes the same piece is both.
Sometimes it is neither and that is the point.
